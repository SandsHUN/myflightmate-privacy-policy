MyFlightMate – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

MyFlightMate ("the app") is published by RZ Dev Studios. It is an offline in-flight companion: it shows flight telemetry from the phone's own GPS receiver and sensors, keeps a flight log, and offers games and relaxation sounds for the flight.

What the app does not do

  • It has no user accounts and no sign-in.
  • It does not collect, store or transmit personal data to us or to anyone else.
  • It contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking software of any kind.
  • It makes no network requests except the one described under News below.

Data processed on your device

Location. The app reads the phone's GPS position to show speed, altitude, heading, the places around you and the distance to your destination. Positions are processed in memory. The automatic flight log keeps a summary of each flight (date, duration, distance, maximum altitude and speed, touchdown load) and the list of cities you flew over, in the app's private storage on your phone. Nothing about your position ever leaves the device. With the tracking notification the app keeps reading GPS while the screen is off; closing the app stops it.

Camera. The optional boarding-pass scanner uses the camera to decode the barcode of a boarding pass. The decoded flight, origin and destination are used to set your route; no image is saved and nothing is uploaded.

Sensors. The accelerometer, rotation sensor and barometer (where present) drive the artificial horizon, the G-force meter, turbulence haptics and the cabin-pressure monitor. Readings are used live and are not stored, except the peak values written into a flight-log entry.

Game results and settings. High scores, the pilot passport, trophies and your settings are kept in the app's private storage. Settings → Backup writes them to a file you choose; Restore reads such a file back; Erase progress deletes them. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

News

If you choose Download news, the app fetches the public RSS feeds of the publishers you selected over your internet connection, so you can read the headlines offline later. The request goes to the publishers' servers directly and carries no identifier of yours beyond what any web request carries (your IP address). The downloaded articles are stored on your phone only.
